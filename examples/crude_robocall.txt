# Fictional sample: crude automated robocall
# Deliberately thin: one device, one threat, no verification.

CALLER: Hello, this is the final notice department. Your account is being suspended today.

TARGET: Which account?

CALLER: Your main account. There is an outstanding balance of 89 dollars. Press one now to
pay and keep the account open.

TARGET: Can I pay later?

CALLER: If it is not paid today the account closes and you lose the number. Press one.

TARGET: And if I press one?

CALLER: You will be put through. Press one now. Thank you. Goodbye.
